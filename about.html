Bigshot was built around a simple idea: coffee can be the beginning of something bigger.

A shared table can become a conversation. A conversation can become an idea. And an idea can become something worth creating.

From Rajpur Road in Dehradun, Bigshot creates a space where coffee meets community and everyday café culture meets creative energy.
